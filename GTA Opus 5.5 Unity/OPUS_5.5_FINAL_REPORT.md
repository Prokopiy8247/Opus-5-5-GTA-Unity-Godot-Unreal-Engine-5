# OPUS 5.5 FINAL REPORT — Port Halcyon (Unity GTA-style free-roam sandbox)

Original, missionless open-world sandbox in Unity 6000.6.0f1 (URP, Forward+) on a ~600 × 600 m island city,
"Port Halcyon". Art direction: **Sunlit Low-Poly Coast** — faceted low-poly models with flat colour palettes,
warm sun, cool shadows, emissive windows and neon at night. Every 3D asset (143) was built through the verified
Blender MCP on `localhost:9880` in `UnityOpus5.5GTA.blend` and exported to Unity; textures, audio, UI and the
world layout are generated by project code. No downloaded or third-party assets.

## How to launch
* **Play:** use the Windows or macOS archive from GitHub Releases; local build outputs are `Builds\PortHalcyon-Windows\PortHalcyon.exe` and `Builds/PortHalcyon-macOS/Port Halcyon.app` → spawns on foot outside the Palm Row safehouse in free roam
  (no missions, no menus to click through). Press **F1** for the admin/benchmark menu, **Esc** for pause/controls.
* **Editor:** Unity 6000.6.0f1 → `Assets/GTA/Scenes/PortHalcyon.unity` → Play.
* **Rebuild from Blender exports:** menu `Halcyon/Run Full Pipeline (no build)` then `Halcyon/5 Build Windows Player`
  (or the batch commands in `DEVELOPMENT.md` §2).
* **Validation run:** `PortHalcyon.exe -autotest` (or `-autotest:plane,boat,…` for a subset) → `QA_Screens/`.

## What was built
* **World:** 7 districts, 144 placed buildings (38 Blender building types), 2 094 props, 848 trees/rocks/plants,
  111 road segments with traffic lights and crosswalks, terrain with a 34 m hill, sea, beaches, piers, airfield.
* **Player:** CharacterController state machine (walk/run/sprint/crouch, jump/vault, swim/dive with breath,
  ladders, cover, freefall/parachute, vehicle seats, ragdoll knockdown), third-person/aim/first-person cameras,
  procedural animation of the 17-bone Blender rig, outfits/hair/barber, 7 trainable skills.

  drive-by, damage/deformation/smoke/fire/explosion, lights/horn/siren/radio, Bay 7 mod shop, garage storage.
* **Combat:** 16 weapons, hitscan + projectiles, recoil/spread/reload, attachments + tints, explosions, melee,
  weapon wheel, crosshair, hit markers.
* **Population:** pedestrians with hearing/sight perception, panic/flee/fight, witnesses; 26 moving traffic cars +
  90 parked; police, SWAT, police helicopter; 9 animal species.
* **Systems:** 0–5 wanted level with pursuit/search, busted, death/respawn, day/night (24 min), 5 weather types,
  shops/economy, phone, taxi with trip skip, parachute, diving, wildlife, shooting range, stunt jumps, street race,
  boat rental, rooftop elevators, save/load, HUD/minimap/full map, admin/benchmark menu, procedural audio + radio.

## What is partial (details in FEATURE_MATRIX.md)

## Known bugs / limitations
* Not implemented: trains/transit line, interiors (buildings are solid; shops and the safehouse are used from the door),
  emergency-service response, weapon pickups/drops, GPS route line, PIT manoeuvre, tyre bursts.
* The prop plane climbs gently (arcade lift tuned for low altitudes); the jet uses the same code but was not tested separately.
* The sun goes down early: 18:30 already looks like night.
* Settings are not persisted between launches.
* All QA ran on the Windows player via `-autotest`; the editor play mode was not used.
* In one re-check (run5c) a freshly carjacked car on an open road did not accelerate; the cause was not found (it passed in
  every later run, and the test now logs wheel and contact diagnostics when it happens).
* Parked cars stand on the pavement edge, about 2 m outside the carriageway of the 11 m streets (placement rule chosen so
  that traffic never treats them as obstacles in the lane).
* Bugs found by the automated runs and fixed in segment 5 (for transparency): build-breaking missing scripts (classes not in
  same-named files corrupted `level0`), every vehicle using the sedan definition, wheel colliders created detached from the
  rigidbody, no reverse gear, bicycles unable to move, stolen AI cars still steered by AI, parked cars without gravity,
  police helicopter without a pilot, wanted level ignoring severity/witnesses, Busted never triggering, pedestrians deaf to
  gunfire, NPCs frozen after leaving cars, taxi destroying itself with the player inside, garage duplicates, plane unable to
  reach take-off speed (hull friction + drag) and inverted pitch, a stale knockdown re-enabling the player's colliders inside
  vehicles, helicopter model standing on its tail, magenta rain, misplaced minimap blips, missing crosshair, menu text residue,
  interaction points inside solid buildings, parked cars spawning on the player, the traffic AI problems described above.

## Wanted / police implementation
* Crimes (gunfire, assault, murder, cop assault/kill, carjack, police-car theft, explosions, hit-and-run, ramming,
  robbery) generate heat = severity × witness factor (police in range 1.3, civilians in sight ≈ 1, unwitnessed 0.15–0.35);
  1 heat = 1 star, reports arrive after a short delay.
* Police response by level: 1★ 2 cars, 2★ 4, 3★ 6 + roadblocks + helicopter (pilot + marksmen), 4★ 8 + SWAT vans,
  5★ 11. Units spawn out of view on roads, pursue with sirens, officers dismount and shoot, passengers shoot from cars at 3★+.
* Pursuit vs search: units chase only while someone (or the helicopter) sees the player; otherwise they patrol around the
  last known position; the escape timer (18–42 s per star) runs only while unseen. Changing clothes and darkness help.
* Busted: at 1–2★ officers close in on an unarmed suspect; standing next to an officer for ~1.4 s → fine + release at the
  police station. Death → hospital bill.

## Performance notes
Windows player at 1600×900 on an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5070 (Windows 11), measured by the autotest over all the tests
(teleports, police chases, explosions, flight): final full run (run7) average **166 FPS**, lowest 0.5 s window
**104 FPS**, no frame longer than 120 ms after warm-up; run5/run6 averaged 162/161 FPS (min 93/91). An early long run
logged one 0.5 s window at 9 FPS, which did not reappear in later runs. The FPS counter and coordinates can be shown in game from the F1 admin menu.
Techniques: NPC update tiers (45 m / 110 m), spawn/despawn radii for peds, traffic, animals and police; parked cars
kinematic until the player is within 45 m; capped real-time lights (8 headlights, 4 sirens, 56 street lamps); pooled
VFX/tracers/decals/audio; static batching + LODGroup culling for the world.
